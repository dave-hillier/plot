Please star ⭐️ the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/dave-hillier/plot) to show your support!

## Getting help

We recommend asking for help on [GitHub discussions](https://github.com/dave-hillier/plot/discussions).

Strive for a [minimal, reproducible example](https://stackoverflow.com/help/minimal-reproducible-example) — it helps people hone in on your problem more quickly.

When asking for help, don’t just post your code and ask people to fix it. Provide context, and say what you want help with. For example:

- What are you trying to achieve? What is your goal?
- What other solutions have you tried?
- What behavior are you currently seeing?
- Is the current behavior not what you expect?

If you think you’ve found a bug in Replot, please file a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/dave-hillier/plot/issues). If the bug also reproduces with [Observable Plot](https://github.com/observablehq/plot), it may be inherited from upstream — noting that in the issue helps.

## Getting involved

We’d love for you to join in! Here are some ways to participate:

* Upvote 👍 or comment on [GitHub issues](https://github.com/dave-hillier/plot/issues). We’d love your input on what to build next. If your desired feature isn’t already there, or if you’ve found a bug, file an issue and tell us about it.

* Answer questions or participate in discussions on [GitHub](https://github.com/dave-hillier/plot/discussions). You’ll help others, and might learn something yourself, too.

* Open a pull request! Read the [guide to contributing](https://github.com/dave-hillier/plot/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## The Observable Plot community

Replot is based on [Observable Plot](https://observablehq.com/plot/), and the concepts — marks, scales, transforms, projections — are shared. The upstream community is a great resource for learning them: the [Plot gallery](https://observablehq.com/@observablehq/plot-gallery), the [Observable forum](https://talk.observablehq.com), and the [Observable community Slack](https://observablehq.com/slack/join).

Please help us maintain a positive environment for all by adhering to the [code of conduct](https://github.com/observablehq/.github/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Thank you!
